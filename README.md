# Yard Management Platform

> **Domain:** Logistics

## Overview

High-volume yards struggle with congestion, inconsistent gate processes, and poor visibility across arrival, departure, dispatch, and inspections. Manual yard checks and spreadsheet-based reconciliation delay decisions and make it hard to locate trailers, allocate docks, or prioritize moves. Leaders lack timely KPIs to control dwell time, carrier turnarounds, and compliance. Without unified, real-time visibility aligning with TMS/WMS workflows, operations absorb overtime, demurrage, and safety risk while throughput stalls. The business needed configurable workflows, accurate status tracking, and live reporting so supervisors could act in minutes not hours and continuously improve yard efficiency.

## Approach

- Mapped end-to-end yard processes (arrival, departure, dispatch, checks, inspections, reconciliation) with operations; defined KPIs such as dwell time, dock utilization, and on-time departure
- Captured data scope from gate logs, checklists, and system events; standardized entities (trailer, dock, move, exception) and statuses
- Customized workflows and user stories per client to mirror real-world roles, SLAs, and approval paths; documented variations for easy configuration
- Designed real-time updates and live dashboards to surface queue lengths, turn times, and exceptions; enabled drill-downs for supervisors
- Implemented validation and reconciliation rules to reduce data gaps; added audit trails for compliance and post-shift reviews
- Delivered iteratively with short sprints, show-and-tell demos, UAT; trained users and rolled out reporting for continuous improvement
